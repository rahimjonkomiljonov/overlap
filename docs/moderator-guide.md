# Overlap moderator guide

One page for Overlap moderators. The full process is in `docs/moderation-playbook.md`; this
is what you need day to day.

## Your job in one sentence

Keep people safe: check the queues every day, act on urgent reports within 2 hours, and
write a clear reason for every decision.

## Where things are

Sign in on Overlap with your campus email, then open `/admin`.

| Page | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Reports** (`/admin/reports`) | Review reports and take an action. Urgent ones are sorted to the top and show a deadline |
| **Verification** (`/admin/verification`) | Approve or reject selfies, so real students get verified |
| **Trust appeals** (`/admin/trust`) | Answer people who appeal an automatic reach limit |

## How fast

| Priority | Examples | Respond within |
|---|---|---|
| **Urgent** | Intimate images shared without consent, deepfakes, illegal filming, threats of violence, someone who seems under 19, anything that sounds like danger right now | **2 hours, any time, including nights and weekends** |
| **Standard** | Harassment, contact after a block, scams, spam, fake profiles, rude content | **24 hours** |

Check the queues at least twice a day: morning and evening. Agree with the other moderator
on who covers which nights and weekends.

## Handling a report

1. **Read the report and the evidence.** The evidence is the messages the reporter chose to
   attach. This is the **only** way you ever see chat. Never look for chats any other way.
2. **Check the person's history** (prior strikes are shown on the report).
3. **Pick one action and write a reason.** The reason is shown to the person, so keep it
   factual and polite.

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| **Dismiss** | Nothing broke the rules. Dismissed reports never count against anyone |
| **Warn** | First, minor problem |
| **Suspend (7 days)** | A repeat problem, or one clearly serious incident |
| **Ban** | Third strike, or a serious urgent case (see below) |
| **Police referral** | Marks an urgent sexual-crime case for evidence preservation (see below) |

The usual order is **warn → 7-day suspension → ban**. A proven urgent case (intimate images,
deepfakes, illegal filming, credible threats) can go straight to a ban.

## Urgent sexual-crime reports

1. **Tell the admin (Rahimjon) right away.** Never handle these alone.
2. **Don't wait to protect people.** If the report is believable, ban or suspend while you
   decide the rest.
3. **Make sure the reporter has the support contacts:** 1366 (women's emergency line), the
   digital sex-crime victim support center, and the Gachon Human Rights Center
   (031-750-8964). They're on the app's safety card.
4. **Going to the police is the victim's choice.** We keep the evidence ready if police ask
   through proper legal channels; we don't contact them on the victim's behalf unasked.

## Selfie verification

Approve when the selfie clearly shows a real person who matches the profile photos.
Reject (with a short reason the person will see) when it's blurry, a photo of a screen, a
different person, or doesn't match. Selfies are deleted automatically after review.

## Trust appeals

Some accounts rank lower in picks after several reports (never a ban). The page shows the
counts and the person's appeal. **Accept** if the reports look like a misunderstanding or a
pile-on; **reject** if the pattern is real. Either way, write a short answer: the person
sees it.

## Rules you must never break

- **Never** look at chats except the evidence attached to a report.
- **Never** tell anyone who reported them.
- **Never** share anything you see as a moderator: no screenshots, no talking about cases
  with friends. Treat it like a doctor treats patient files.
- **Never** suspend or ban without a written reason.
- **Never** moderate someone you know personally; hand it to the other moderator.

## Look after yourself

Some reports are upsetting. Take breaks, swap urgent duty with the other moderator when you
need to, and talk to the admin if something stays with you. The Gachon counseling center is
031-750-5965.
