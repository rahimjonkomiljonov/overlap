# Overlap test guide (for the two testers)

You are two of the first real people to use Overlap. The goal is to find anything that
breaks, confuses you or looks wrong, **before** other students see it. Nothing you do is
graded. Breaking things is the point.

**Time:** about 45 minutes. **Where:** on your phone, at https://overlaps.tech/en/login
(Korean is https://overlaps.tech/login).

## Before you start

You need:

- Your **@gachon.ac.kr** email address (only campus emails work).
- An **invite code**. The owner sends it to you privately. It works once and only for you.
- Your phone. **Tester A uses Safari (iPhone). Tester B uses Samsung Internet or opens the
  link inside KakaoTalk.** This checks both kinds of browsers.
- Two people online at the same time for steps 5 to 8.

Good to know:

- The sign-in code arrives by email. **Check your Spam or Junk folder.** A new sender is
  often filtered at first. If a code does not show up after 5 minutes, tell the owner,
  who can find it.
- Use only fake test content. No real photos of faces you would not want stored. Any
  pictures are fine, three of them.
- Photos and selfies in this test are real uploads to the live system. The owner will
  delete your test accounts at the end.

## How to report a problem

For each problem, send the owner (a message is fine):

1. The step number.
2. What you expected, and what happened.
3. A screenshot of the whole screen.
4. Your phone and browser (for example "iPhone, Safari").

A "boring" result (everything worked) is useful too. Write **OK** next to each step as
you go.

## The steps

### 1. Sign up

1. Open https://overlaps.tech/en/login.
2. Enter your campus email and request a code.
3. Check: **does the code email arrive within a minute or two?** (Look in Spam too.)
4. Enter the code.
5. Enter your invite code when asked.

Check: the wording is clear, nothing is cut off, buttons are easy to tap.

### 2. Finish your profile

Go through every screen: basics, what you are here for, courses, clubs, spots, free time,
interests, values quiz, prompts, photos.

- **Pick "Friends" or "Study"** as what you are here for (Dating is not open yet).
- Add **at least 3 prompts** and **at least 3 photos**.

Check each of these and report if one fails:

- Dropdown lists are readable (you can see every option).
- Lists such as interests, courses and clubs show choices **before** you type, and
  narrow as you type.
- Your photos show up after you upload them.
- The last screen ("How others see you") shows what you entered, not "0%" or empty.
- Pressing **Finish** takes you to the main screen. Pressing **Back** never loses what
  you typed.

### 3. Verify yourself with a selfie

1. Open **Verify** and take a selfie.
2. Wait. The owner approves it by hand.
3. Check: after approval, your profile shows as verified.

Tell the owner when you have submitted your selfie.

### 4. See your picks

After both of you are verified, the owner starts the daily matching. Then open **Picks**.

Check: **you can see each other** (you both picked a shared course or club, so you
should). Is it clear why someone was suggested? Do the cards look right?

### 5. Wave

1. Tester A: open Tester B's card and **wave**.
2. Tester B: you should get the wave. Wave back.
3. Check: **you now have a match.**

### 6. Chat

1. Open the match and send each other two messages.
2. Check: **messages appear without reloading the page**, in the right order, quickly.

### 7. Plan a meetup

1. Propose a time and place (use a campus spot).
2. The other person accepts.
3. Share the meetup link and **open it in a private/incognito window**.

Check: the shared link shows only the safe details (time and place), nothing private.

### 8. Report and block

1. In the chat, **report** the other person (use "test" as the reason).
2. Tell the owner. They should see the report in the admin page right away.
3. **Block** the other person.
4. Check: **they disappear** from your picks, waves and matches, and you disappear from
   theirs.

### 9. Your data

1. Open **Privacy** (from your profile) and **export your data**. Check that you get a
   file that is yours and readable.
2. **Delete your account** (Tester B only, or both at the end).

## Also look for, on every screen

- Text that is cut off, overlapping, or too small to read.
- A page that scrolls sideways.
- Buttons that do nothing when tapped, or need two taps.
- Words in the wrong language, or English and Korean mixed.
- Anything slow (more than about 5 seconds with no sign of progress).
- Anything that makes you feel unsafe or uncomfortable using it.
- Dark mode and light mode: switch your phone setting and look again.

## When you are done

Tell the owner:

- How many steps were OK and which had problems.
- The one thing that confused you most.
- Whether you would use this with real friends.

Thank you. Your feedback decides when other students are let in.
